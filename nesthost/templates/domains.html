Domain ka sach

Koi bhi platform asli .com / .in domain free mein register nahi kar sakta, kyunki registry ko fees deni padti hain. Hostinger bhi domain paid plan ke saath dikhata hai. Free mein ye raaste hain:

1. Free subdomain (yahin)

yourname.{{ base }}: instantly free, HTTPS ke saath. Signup karo.

2. Apna domain, sasta

Naye extensions (.xyz, .site, .online) aksar pehle saal bahut sasti sale mein milte hain. Kisi bhi registrar par price compare karo, phir yahan connect karo. Renewal price zaroor dekho.

3. Student ya community free offers

Kuch registrars students ya developers ko pehle saal free domain dete hain (jaise GitHub Student Developer Pack ke offers), aur kuch community projects free subdomains dete hain. Offers badalte rehte hain, isliye current terms khud check karo.

4. Domain check tool

Koi bhi domain likho aur dekho ki wo taken hai ya nahi (RDAP se).

Testing ke liye bina domain

Local par mysite.localhost khud chalta hai. Server par demo ke liye BASE_DOMAIN=1-2-3-4.sslip.io (apni IP ke saath) free wildcard DNS deta hai.
